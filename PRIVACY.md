# Privacy Policy

Nocturne Reader respects your privacy.

## Data Collection

Nocturne Reader does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information.

The extension processes webpage content locally on the user's device only for the purpose of providing dark mode functionality.

Nocturne Reader does not transmit browsing history, webpage content, or other user data to the developer or to third parties.

## Local Storage

Nocturne Reader may store user preferences, such as whether Dark Mode is enabled, locally on the user's device. This information is not transmitted off the device.

## Third-Party Services

Nocturne Reader does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party tracking services.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the Issues section of the Nocturne Reader GitHub repository.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of Nocturne Reader change.
